About Raquel

Raquel Lowell is a Graphic Designer living in San Francisco. She's a generalist with deep knowledge in branding, web design, and experiential environments. This breadth of skills is ideal for non-profit organizations and startups in a growth phase.

She graduated from Cal State Long Beach with a BA in Graphic Design. For the last decade, Raquel has provided data driven and human centered design solutions to clients in tech, finance, and consumer packaged goods.

Raquel is passionate about the arts, yoga, and improving the lives of marginalized communities. When she's not at her computer, she's in her stuido. Visit her Fine Art Portfolio.
